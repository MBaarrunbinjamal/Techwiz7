LOGO SETUP - two steps to finish

1. Place the assets folder at your project ROOT (same level as lib and pubspec.yaml).
   The logo file must end up at:  assets/logo.png

2. Open pubspec.yaml and add the asset under the flutter: section.
   Match the exact indentation below (two spaces before flutter, four before assets):

flutter:
  uses-material-design: true
  assets:
    - assets/logo.png

If your pubspec already has a flutter: block, only add the assets: lines inside it.
Then run:  flutter pub get

Logo now shows on: Splash, Login, Choose, Register app bar, Email verification.
Category and stat savings icons were left unchanged.

Note: this changes the in-app logo only. The app launcher icon (home screen)
lives in android/ and ios/, not in lib. To change that too, use the
flutter_launcher_icons package with assets/logo.png as the source.
